<Item title={"1. How to Choose the Right Service Provider?"}>

Before selecting an IT asset disposal provider, businesses should ask several questions.

</Item>
<Item title={"2. Does the provider handle the type of equipment you need to retire?"}>

Make sure the service covers your laptops, desktops, servers, storage devices, printers, or other relevant equipment.

</Item>
<Item title={"3. How is data handled?"}>

Ask about the provider’s data-erasure or destruction process and how sensitive information is protected.

</Item>
<Item title={"4. Is asset tracking available?"}>

For business environments, clear documentation and asset identification can be valuable.

</Item>
<Item title={"5. How is e-waste handled?"}>

E-waste is handled by collecting, dismantling, and recycling electronic items through authorized recyclers or manufacturer take-back programs, while unsafe informal methods still dominate in many places.

</Item>
<Item title={"6. What documentation is provided?"}>

Depending on your organization’s requirements, disposal records and data-erasure documentation may be important.

</Item>
<Item title={"7. Can the provider support bulk requirements?"}>

Companies replacing dozens or hundreds of devices may need a provider capable of handling larger volumes efficiently.
