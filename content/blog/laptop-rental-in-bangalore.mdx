**Laptop Rental vs Buying: Cost Considerations:**

Cost is one of the most important factors when comparing rental and purchase.

With buying, the business pays the purchase price upfront. It may then incur additional expenses over the laptop’s lifecycle, such as:

-   Repairs
-   Maintenance
-   Warranty extensions
-   Battery replacement
-   Hardware upgrades
-   IT support
-   Asset management
-   Replacement costs
-   Depreciation
-   Resale or disposal

With rental, the business pays according to the agreed rental arrangement.

However, rental is not automatically cheaper in every situation. If a laptop is required continuously for many years, purchasing may provide better long-term value.

Therefore, businesses should compare the total cost for the expected usage period rather than comparing only the purchase price with the rental rate.
